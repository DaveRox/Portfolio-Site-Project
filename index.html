About

Whatever money question keeps you up at night, odds are I've faced it myself — from both sides of the table.

I've been a Marine helicopter mechanic, a stockbroker, a commodities trader, a real estate managing partner, and a business owner who rebuilt after Katrina. I've made payroll, ridden out volatile markets, started over in a new country, and turned a failing operation profitable. When you tell me about your business, your risk, or your worry about what comes next, it isn't theoretical to me.

Today I'm the Director of Development for New York Life's New Orleans General Office — the person responsible for training every agent in the office. Before that, I was an agent, financial planner, and registered investment adviser myself. My designations cover the full arc of a financial life: comprehensive planning, life insurance, retirement income, long-term care, and leadership. If your question touches money, family, or a business, it almost certainly lands inside work I do every day.

And you'll always get it straight. I teach plain-English, client-first advice for a living — no jargon, no pressure. I also keep a close eye on how AI and decentralized systems are reshaping finance, because the advice business is changing, and staying ahead of that curve is part of the job.

Background
1987 – 1992
United States Marine Corps

Served as a helicopter mechanic and AH-1W plane captain. Gulf War veteran. The discipline, accountability, and training-first mindset forged here still drive everything that follows.

1992 – 1995
Series 7 Stockbroker · San Diego

Started in the financial industry as a licensed Series 7 stockbroker — the beginning of more than three decades of markets, trading, and client-focused financial work.

1997 – 1999
Series 3 Commodities Trader · San Diego

Traded commodities as a Series 3 licensed professional, building deep first-hand experience in futures markets, risk, and the discipline it takes to navigate volatility.

2002 – 2005
Managing Partner · Harborside Property Management · New Orleans

Led a successful real estate portfolio company as Managing Partner — adding property, asset management, and partnership operations to a growing base of hands-on business experience.

2006 – 2008
Post-Katrina Entrepreneur · New Orleans

Founded True South Yacht Services in the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina. Helped clients salvage boats from New Orleans marinas, then built the company into the second-largest yacht brokerage in Louisiana by sales volume.

2008 – 2013
Economics Editor & Finance Director · Paris

Covered the Global Financial Crisis as it unfolded, helping readers make sense of the chaos and navigate what it meant for their money — along with the Greek debt crisis, the future of the Euro, and an exposé on the Madoff scandal — for Carnet Atlantique, a Franco-American quarterly. Simultaneously managed finance and IT for the US Embassy Association, reversing over a decade of annual losses and reaching net profitability within two years.

2013 – 2017
Head of Finance & Technology · KRW International · Paris/New Orleans

Ran the finance and technology departments for KRW International, a global consulting firm built around leadership Character — the research behind the Harvard Business Review Press study Return on Character®. Started in Paris and brought the role home to New Orleans. Led a cloud migration that cut costs and strengthened data security, and introduced the CRM that transformed sales operations.

2016 – 2019
Head Coach, LEAN Launchpad · Loyola University New Orleans

Coached MBA candidates in LEAN startup methodology, working with fellow business leaders and subject matter experts to commercialize proprietary technologies provided by NASA program partners.

2019 – 2023
Advisory Board Member · Gilded

Served on the advisory board of Gilded, an open-finance platform for business built on blockchain infrastructure, through the company's acquisition by Bitwave, the enterprise digital-asset finance platform. An early front-row seat to the intersection of decentralized finance and enterprise operations.

2017 – Present
New York Life · New Orleans

Began as an agent in the Greater New Orleans area, then moved through roles as a registered representative and financial planner before stepping into leadership as Development Manager and, today, Director of Development.

About

As Director of Development for New York Life's New Orleans General Office, I give every Agent I train the best possible chance to succeed.

My work spans the full range of professional development: sales training and licensing, financial solution design, prospecting techniques, and implementation of New York Life's curriculum. I also advise and consult with management on recruiting and retention — because building a great office starts before the first client meeting.

Before moving into development, I spent years as an agent, financial planner, and registered investment adviser. The winding path that got me here — from New Orleans to Paris and back — is below.

Outside the office, I follow the collision of AI and decentralized systems closely — because the same forces reshaping finance and technology are reshaping the advice business. Staying ahead of that curve isn't optional.

Background
1987 – 1992
United States Marine Corps

Served as a helicopter mechanic and AH-1W plane captain. Gulf War veteran. The discipline, accountability, and training-first mindset forged here still drive everything that follows.

1992 – 1995
Series 7 Stockbroker · San Diego

Started in the financial industry as a licensed Series 7 stockbroker — the beginning of more than three decades of markets, trading, and client-focused financial work.

1997 – 1999
Series 3 Commodities Trader · San Diego

Traded commodities as a Series 3 licensed professional, building deep first-hand experience in futures markets, risk, and the discipline it takes to navigate volatility.

2002 – 2005
Managing Partner · Harborside Property Management · New Orleans

Led a successful real estate portfolio company as Managing Partner — adding property, asset management, and partnership operations to a growing base of hands-on business experience.

2006 – 2008
Post-Katrina Entrepreneur · New Orleans

Founded True South Yacht Services in the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina. Helped clients salvage boats from New Orleans marinas, then built the company into the second-largest yacht brokerage in Louisiana by sales volume.

2010 – 2014
Economics Editor & Finance Director · Paris

Wrote on the Greek debt crisis and the future of the Euro for Carnet Atlantique, a Franco-American quarterly. Simultaneously managed finance and IT for the US Embassy Association, reversing over a decade of annual losses and reaching net profitability within two years.

2016 – 2019
Head Coach, LEAN Launchpad · Loyola University New Orleans

Coached MBA candidates in LEAN startup methodology, working with fellow business leaders and subject matter experts to commercialize proprietary technologies provided by NASA program partners.

2017
Joined New York Life · New Orleans

Began as an agent in the Greater New Orleans area, then moved through roles as a registered representative and financial planner before stepping into leadership as Development Manager and, today, Director of Development.

2019 – 2023
Advisory Board Member · Gilded

Served on the advisory board of Gilded, an open-finance platform for business built on blockchain infrastructure. An early front-row seat to the intersection of decentralized finance and enterprise operations.

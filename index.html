About

As Director of Development for New York Life's New Orleans General Office, I give every Agent I train the best possible chance to succeed.

My work spans the full range of professional development: sales training and licensing, financial solution design, prospecting techniques, and implementation of New York Life's curriculum. I also advise and consult with management on recruiting and retention — because building a great office starts before the first client meeting.

Before moving into development, I spent years as an agent, financial planner, and investment adviser — and before that I coached MBA candidates at Loyola in LEAN startup methodology, advised an open-finance startup, and ran my own companies. I bring the full picture to the table.

Outside the office, I follow the collision of AI and decentralized systems closely — because the same forces reshaping finance and technology are reshaping the advice business. Staying ahead of that curve isn't optional.

Credentials

CLF® · Chartered Leadership Fellow

The American College of Financial Services — Advanced leadership designation for financial services managers. Focuses on building, developing, and retaining high-performing teams in a competitive field.

—
ChFC® · Chartered Financial Consultant

The American College of Financial Services — Comprehensive financial planning designation covering advanced wealth management, taxation, insurance, and estate planning strategies.

—
CLU® · Chartered Life Underwriter

The American College of Financial Services — The gold standard in life insurance planning. Covers risk management, business succession, and wealth transfer strategies for individuals and families.

—
RICP® · Retirement Income Certified Professional

The American College of Financial Services — Specialized in retirement income planning, covering strategies for generating sustainable income, managing sequence-of-returns risk, and maximizing Social Security benefits.

—
CLTC® · Certification in Long Term Care

Certification for Long Term Care — Focused on the financial and human impact of extended care needs. Helps clients protect assets and plan ahead for the realities of aging and chronic illness.

—
FSCP® · Financial Services Certified Professional

The American College of Financial Services — Foundational designation covering client-centered financial planning, ethical standards, and the practical application of financial solutions across life stages.
